Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Scribd Downloader Bot (@scribdnxprobot) collects, uses, and safeguards information when you interact with our Telegram bot and website services.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our Telegram bot, we only process basic identifiers necessary to deliver the service:

  • Telegram User ID: A numerical identifier assigned by Telegram to associate your account with your subscription status and download count.
  • Telegram Username & First Name: Used exclusively to address you in messages and log support activity.
  • Requested Document Links: URLs submitted to process document unlocks and reader links.
  • Verification Screenshots: Photos voluntarily sent by users to prove Instagram follow status for activating free 1-month passes.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information solely for:

  • Providing document reading and downloading services.
  • Tracking subscription validity (e.g., 30-day free access periods).
  • Preventing abuse, spam, and enforcing fair rate limits.
  • Delivering important service announcements and bot status updates.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely in encrypted databases. We implement standard security practices to prevent unauthorized access. We do not sell, rent, or share personal user data with any third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. Third-Party Services

Our service interacts with the following external platforms:

  • Telegram: For bot communication under Telegram's Privacy Policy.
  • Instagram: To confirm follow status via user-submitted verification screenshots.
  • Scribd: To fetch publicly accessible embed content.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your interaction history or user profile from our database at any time. Simply contact the administrator on Telegram: @nxproo.

6. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

7. Contact Information
